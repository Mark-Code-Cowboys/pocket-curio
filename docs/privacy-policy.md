# Pocket Curio Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** (set at publication)
**Publisher:** Code Cowboys LLC
**Published at:** https://pocketcurio.app/privacy/ (code-cowboys.com/privacy/pocketcurio redirects here)

Pocket Curio is a personal journal of souvenirs and the places they
came from. Our privacy policy is short because the app is built so that
we *cannot* see your data.

## The short version

Everything you record in Pocket Curio — photos, places, dates, who gave
you what, the memories — is stored **only on your device**. Pocket
Curio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no
network calls. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any
personal data. We could not hand your collection to anyone even if
asked, because we never have it.

## Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you photograph a
souvenir or a shelf of them. The photos you take are stored locally
with the rest of your collection and are never uploaded — there is
nowhere to upload them to.

## Reading the print on a souvenir

When you photograph a souvenir, Pocket Curio reads the text printed on
it **on your device** (Google ML Kit's bundled, offline text
recognition) and offers it as the place name for you to check. The
photo and everything read from it stay on your phone.

## Location

Pocket Curio requests **no location permission**. The map is drawn
from the state and country you type; a souvenir's coordinates exist
only if you enter them yourself, and are stored locally like everything
else.

## Sharing, export, and backup

CSV export and the backup file are created only when you tap the
button, and go only where you send them via your phone's share sheet.
Restoring a backup reads a file you choose. We are never in that path.

## Purchases

Pocket Curio Pro is sold through Google Play / the App Store. The
stores process payment and tell the app only "this purchase is owned."
We receive no payment details and keep no purchase records of our own.

## Children

Pocket Curio does not knowingly collect data from anyone — including
children — because it collects no data at all.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes what leaves your device, this policy
and its effective date will change with it, and the app will say so
before anything new happens.
