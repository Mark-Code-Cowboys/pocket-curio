### App Store

> A souvenir is a place + a memory. Pocket Curio is the shelf you can carry: photograph each keychain, magnet, or shot glass, name where it's from, and keep the story that came home with it.
>
> WHAT'S IN 1.0
> • Camera first. Take the photo and the place name is read straight off the souvenir for you to check. Two fields, under ten seconds.
> • Shelf scan. Already have a fridge full? One photo of the whole thing, box each souvenir with your finger, and Pocket Curio turns every box into its own photo, reads the place off it, and adds them all at once.
> • Collections. One phone, the whole household: the keychain collector and the magnet collector each get their own shelf, sorted by date or by place.
> • The memory. Who gave it to you, the trip, the day, a rating, and the story.
> • The map (Pro). Every state and country your souvenirs came from, lit up one place at a time. Souvenirs by year. The oldest one you own.
> • Backup and export (Pro). A full backup you can restore on any phone, and a CSV of everything.
> • Private by construction. No account, no cloud, no analytics. Photos and the text read from them never leave your phone.
>
> Your first collection and your first 25 souvenirs are free forever. Pocket Curio Pro is one purchase or month to month.
>
